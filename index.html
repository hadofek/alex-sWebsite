About

A guitarist first.
A teacher second.

I've been playing electric guitar for six years — performing with bands, writing music, obsessing over tone, making mistakes, starting over. My roots are in metal and rock, but what I've learned applies everywhere, because most of what matters about the instrument isn't genre-specific.

I teach online because geography shouldn't limit who gets good instruction. If you're serious about learning, I want to work with you — wherever you are, whatever level, whatever music you care about.

The Journey

Where are you now?

01

Starting out

You've never touched an electric guitar, or you have, and it didn't go anywhere. We begin at the beginning — how to hold the instrument, how to make it sound like something, how to enjoy the process without faking it. The goal isn't to get you through a syllabus. It's to make you want to pick it up again tomorrow.

02

Breaking through

You know some chords. You can play a few riffs. And then somewhere around there, you stopped improving. This is the most common place people get stuck, and the most interesting place to teach. We work on what's actually holding you back — usually not technique, usually not talent. Usually a missing piece of theory, or a habit that formed too early.

03

Going deep

You already play. What you want now is to play better — to understand why certain things work, to develop your own tone and voice, to go into territory you haven't been able to reach alone. Sweep picking, odd time signatures, improvisation, composition. We go as far as you want to go.
